# ITSM / ITIL-aware Tier 1 Ticket and SLA SOP

**Status:** practical service-management model, not an ITIL certification claim.

## Classification

- **Incident:** unplanned interruption or degradation of an IT service.
- **Service request:** standard request such as access, onboarding, device setup or information.
- **Security event/incident:** suspicious activity requiring the approved security escalation path.

## Priority model used in this pack

| Priority | Example | Target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Security compromise or critical business outage | 1 hour |
| P2 | Major user-impact issue / onboarding blocker | 4 hours |
| P3 | Standard operational incident | 8 hours |
| P4 | Low-impact request/content update | 24 hours |

These are synthetic lab targets, not GreenCo's actual SLA values.

## Ticket lifecycle

1. Log requester, impact, symptoms, device/service and timestamp.
2. Categorise incident/request and assign priority based on impact/urgency.
3. Acknowledge and begin investigation within the applicable SLA.
4. Capture evidence before disruptive changes.
5. Resolve within Tier 1 authority or escalate with useful diagnostics.
6. Follow up; do not treat escalation as abandonment of ownership.
7. Verify with the user/system.
8. Record resolution and close accurately.

## Escalation quality

A useful escalation contains scope, impact, timestamps, exact error, steps already tried, evidence collected, suspected layer/cause, user availability and required next action.

## ITIL awareness demonstrated

This pack uses service-management concepts including incident vs request distinction, prioritisation, SLA tracking, escalation, knowledge documentation, asset/configuration records and continual improvement. It does not claim ITIL certification.
