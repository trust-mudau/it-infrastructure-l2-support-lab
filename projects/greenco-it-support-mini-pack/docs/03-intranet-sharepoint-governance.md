# SharePoint / Intranet Content Governance

**Status:** scenario design; no live GreenCo or Microsoft SharePoint site was modified.

## Proposed information structure

- Home
  - announcements
  - quick links
  - service/help links
- Departments
  - Finance
  - Commercial
  - People Operations
  - Digital and Data
- Policies and SOPs
- Forms and templates
- IT help and security awareness

## Update workflow

1. Department submits content/update request.
2. Confirm content owner and required approval.
3. Check title, document version, effective date and target audience.
4. Update page/link/document location in the approved area.
5. Test navigation and permissions from a normal-user perspective.
6. Record change date, owner and approver.
7. Remove superseded/duplicate content when approved.

## Governance principles

- Prefer clear ownership for every page/document.
- Avoid publishing unapproved or sensitive content.
- Keep navigation shallow and predictable.
- Use consistent naming and version information.
- Review stale links and outdated announcements regularly.
- Use group-based access where practical rather than uncontrolled direct permissions.
- Escalate external-sharing, sensitivity-label, retention or compliance questions beyond Tier 1 authority.
